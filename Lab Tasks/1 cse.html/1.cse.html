Classic Margherita Pizza

A timeless Italian recipe from Napples

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 12 min   Serves: 4

About This Recipe

The Margherita is the king of Neapolitan pizza — simple ingredients, bold flavors. Named after Queen Margherita of Italy in 1889, it represents the colors of the Italian flag: red tomato, white mozzarella, and green basil.

Ingredients

  • 500g bread flour
  • 325ml warm water
  • 7g active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 400g San Marzano tomatoes
  • 250g fresh mozzarella
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Extra virgin olive oil for drizzling

Instructions

  1. Make the dough: Dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water. Let it sit for 5 minutes until foamy. Mix flour and salt, add yeast mixture and olive oil. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  2. First rise: Place dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover with a damp cloth. Let rise in a warm spot for 1-2 hours until doubled in size.
  3. Preheat: Set your oven to the highest setting, around 250°C / 500°F.
  4. Shape the dough: Divide dough into 4 equal portions. Stretch each piece into a thin round using your hands.
  5. Top the pizza: Spread crushed San Marzano tomatoes evenly. Tear mozzarella into pieces and distribute over the sauce. Add a few basil leaves.
  6. Bake: Slide onto the hot stone or baking sheet. Bake for 10-12 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  7. Serve: Drizzle with extra virgin olive oil, add fresh basil, and serve immediately.

Tip: For the most authentic result, use tipo 00 flour and let your dough cold ferment in the fridge for 24-72 hours.
